# Welcome to Feedback Kitchen

Feedback Kitchen is a browser-based assessment feedback tool. It is adapted from the original Feedback Kitchen Excel workbook created and shared by Dr Michael Harker at the University of Strathclyde, redesigned as a web application for the University of Waikato and educators worldwide.

The core philosophy is straightforward: build your rubric once, then deliver consistent, personalised feedback at scale.

![Feedback Kitchen overview](images/ch01-feedback-kitchen-overview.png)

### Who it is for

Feedback Kitchen is built for university lecturers, tutors, teaching assistants, markers, and school educators who mark student assessments against structured criteria.

When marking large cohorts or coordinating multi-marker teams, feedback quality frequently degrades toward the end of a long marking session. Feedback Kitchen eliminates repetitive typing while keeping every grading decision in your hands.

### What it is and is not

Feedback Kitchen is not an autograder. It does not read student essays, parse code submissions, or assign grades on its own.

Every grade, override, and written phrase originates from human judgement. The tool automates the mechanical assembly of your feedback: combining tier-level rubric commentary, introductory and closing remarks, score calculations, and personal snippets into a coherent student report.

### Key principles

- **Client-side execution:** The entire application runs inside your browser tab. There are no user accounts, no backend databases, and no tracking servers holding student submissions.
- **Criteria-referenced assessment:** Grading aligns directly with explicit performance descriptors across predefined criteria.
- **Personalisation at scale:** You can inject custom personal snippets and edit assembled drafts inline before sending feedback to students.
- **Data sovereignty:** Your scoring templates, personal snippets, and student cohort records remain on your local device.
