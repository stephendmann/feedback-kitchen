# Formative and Summative Assessment

Feedback Kitchen adapts to both **formative** (developmental feedback on in-progress work) and **summative** (formal grading of final submissions) assessment models.

![Formative vs summative configurations](images/ch29-assessment-types.png)

### Architectural comparison

| Configuration Dimension | Formative Assessment | Summative Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Objective** | Identify developmental priorities and actionable revision steps. | Record audited marks against institutional learning standards. |
| **Grade Scale** | Simplified pass/resubmit scale or standard scale used purely diagnostically. | Full regional grade preset (e.g. NZ University 10-band, UK Degree). |
| **Rubric Descriptors** | Emphasises concrete next steps in Developing and Proficient tiers. | Rigorously distinguishes performance boundaries across all tiers. |
| **Feedback Templates** | Directs students to revision resources, office hours, and academic support. | Provides comprehensive evaluative summary and final performance framing. |
| **Late Penalties** | Typically disabled to encourage timely draft submission without punitive marking. | Standard deduction schedule active (e.g. 10% per day late). |
| **Archival Output** | Formatted PDF slips or plain-text clipboard copy for draft return. | Multi-sheet Cohort Excel Workbooks, Marker Records, and Moderation Exports. |

### Configuring a formative scorer

To build a scorer for essay drafts, project proposals, or lab practice:

1. **Rubric Authoring:** Focus criteria on draft maturity (e.g. *Argument Structure*, *Preliminary Evidence*, *Methodological Plan*).
2. **Action-Oriented Descriptors:** Ensure Developing descriptors provide specific prompts (*"Your draft introduces three case studies; for the final version, narrow this to two and deepen the comparative analysis"*).
3. **Snippet Integration:** Build personal snippets containing links to course writing guidelines, citation style guides, and academic support workshops.
4. **Draft Return:** Use **Copy feedback** to paste remarks into draft discussion forums or click **Print page** to generate a quick PDF feedback slip.

### Configuring a summative scorer

To build a scorer for final exams, term papers, or capstone projects:

1. **Scale Alignment:** Select your institutional grade preset to ensure midpoint calculations align with official university grade thresholds.
2. **Defensible Descriptors:** Ensure criteria boundaries are clearly differentiated to withstand student grade appeals.
3. **Late Deduction Scheduling:** Configure explicit late deduction bands in Step 6 of the builder wizard.
4. **Formal Moderation:** Use **Export cohort (Excel)** for departmental grade submission and **Export for Moderation** for second-marker audits.
