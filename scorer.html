Using the DEMO101 / 2026 Short Written Response scorer

This demo scorer helps you mark a short written response using a clear rubric and generate plain‑language feedback. Use it to practise the workflow before building your own scorer.

  • Student — Add the student name or identifier and any basic details.
  • Rubric scores — Choose the level that best matches each criterion. The scorer calculates the total from the saved weights.
  • Focus marking — Optionally mark one criterion at a time, grade and feedback together.
  • Penalty & grade override — Apply a late-submission penalty or manually override the calculated grade.
  • Editable feedback draft — Review or edit the generated feedback so it matches your judgement and tone.
  • Marker's notes — Keep any private marker notes here (not shown to the student).
  • Finish — Export the student record or print the page.
  • Cohort — Saved students collect here for export and insights.

Finish by checking the score, band, and feedback, then use ↺ New student to mark the next response.

What the assistant cannot change

            
            

              
The following parts are fixed and always come from your own marking:

              
    
                
  • Any scores you have entered
    • 
                
  • Any penalties (for lateness, academic integrity, etc.)
    • 
                
  • The greeting (for example, “Hi”)
    • 
                
  • Intro and closing paragraphs (based on the pre-penalty grade)
    • 
                
  • The TOTAL SCORE line
    • 
                
  • Late submission notice and the final penalised score
    • 
              

              

                These stay exactly as generated from your grades, even if you use assistant suggestions.
                Your professional judgement drives all scores; this tool only helps with wording.
              

            

          


          
          

            
              
What the assistant uses

            
            

              
To draft or refine wording, the assistant only uses:

              
    
                
  • The rubric descriptors for each criterion
    • 
                
  • The scores you have entered so far
    • 
                
  • (Optional) your private marker’s notes and saved snippets
    • 
              

              

                
              

              

              
            

          


          
          

            
              
What happens to my data?

            
            

              

                If you use this assistant, the selected text (rubric descriptors, your scores,
                and any notes or snippets) is sent to the AI service to generate a suggestion.
                If you do not use it, your marking stays entirely local to your browser.
              

              

                Built-in protection: student names and IDs entered in Sections A or B
                are automatically removed from anything sent to the AI service, even if they appear
                in Marker’s Notes or your draft. Email addresses are also stripped.
              

              

                Good practice: still use initials or generic references in Marker’s
                Notes where possible — the scrub is a safety net, not a licence to paste PII.

Enable Moderation Export

      
Privacy-reduced workbook for cross-marker moderation meetings.


      

        

          
Purpose

          A privacy-reduced export to support lecturer-led, pooled, cross-marker moderation conversations. Not for performance management of markers and not for student-level disclosure.
        

        

          
What it includes

          Shuffled rubric scores, totals, grade band, anonymous marker labels (collapsed when n<5), suppression flags, cohort/paper/assessment identifiers, FK version, hour-rounded export timestamp.
        

        

          
What it excludes

          Student names, IDs / NSNs, emails, marker names, marker notes, feedback text, verbatim snippets, exact timestamps, reversible lookup keys, composite marker-quality scores.
        

        

          
Privacy-reduced, not anonymised

          Re-identification may still be possible in small cohorts. The export is blocked if n<15. You remain responsible for how the file is shared and stored.
        

        

          
Lecturer / coordinator responsibilities

          Only enable this if you are the responsible paper coordinator for this assessment. Inform markers that the export is enabled; they will see a notice banner. Disable it again when moderation is complete.
        

      


      

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

      


      

        Moderation export is tied to this exact combination of paper code, cohort ID, and assessment ID. If you change any of these after enabling, the export will appear disabled until you re-open settings and save again.
